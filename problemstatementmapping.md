## Core distinction

Skill Mitra does not operate as a conventional resume-based job portal.

It creates a connected evidence loop in which:

1. Industry defines role-relevant skills.
2. Students assess themselves against those skills.
3. Skill gaps are made visible.
4. Students close gaps through projects, challenges, certifications, and internships.
5. Faculty and industry verify the work.
6. Skill Passports store trustworthy evidence.
7. Companies find candidates using verified capabilities.
8. Institutions use the aggregated data to improve training, curriculum alignment, internships, and placements.

## Stakeholder outcomes

| Stakeholder | Current pain | Skill Mitra outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Students | Unclear role requirements, weak proof of skill, mismatched opportunities | A measurable career path and verified evidence of ability |
| Industry | Resumes do not show real capability; shortlisting is slow and uncertain | Searchable, explainable, verified candidate evidence |
| Academicians | Limited visibility into industry practice and student skill progress | Verification, mentoring, and industry-engagement workflows |
| Institutions | Limited data on skill gaps, internship participation, and placement readiness | Cohort-level insights for intervention and curriculum improvement |
